`strategy:enabled-state`, `trade:metrics:*`) and the monitoring-service API. Read-only.

`strategy:enabled-state` = preclose-continuation **true**, monday-strength **true**, gapup-fade **true**.
All other strategies false. Correct.

## 2. Did it fire when it should have? **NO — PCC is missing ~2/3 of its trades**

| strategy | fired | expected | rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| PRECLOSE_CONTINUATION | 7 | 19 sessions (it is a DAILY 15:00 ET trade) | **37%** |
| MONDAY_STRENGTH | 4 | 4 Mondays | 100% ✅ |

Production held NQ candles for **every** session in the window, including the 5 sessions with no
alert record at all (07-23, 07-28, 07-31, 08-06, 08-13) — so data was flowing and PCC still did not
fire. (Caveat: PCC has a TRIN/breadth conditioner that can legitimately gate entries; that must be
checked against the live conditioner state before concluding all 12 misses are faults.)

## 3. Did it fire correctly? **Partly — two defects, both self-corrected mid-window**

- **Late stamps.** PCC should stamp 15:00:01 ET. Signals from 07-24 → 08-05 stamp **19:01:02 UTC =
  15:01 ET, one minute late**. From 08-11 they stamp 15:00:03 — correct. Monday shows the same drift:
  09:31 → 09:30 → 09:29 ET across the window (09:29 is correct).
- **Stale signal prices.** The price on the signal does not match the market at the stamp. Worst case
  08-03 MONDAY: signal price 28419 vs 28349 in the candle at that moment — **70 points**. Consistent
  with the signal carrying the decision-bar price while firing minutes later.
- Symbol switched NQU6 → MNQU6 around 08-10 (micros).

## 5. Infrastructure defects found

- **`schwab_auth_expired` ×14 on 07-27**, again 08-07 and ×5 on 08-14. Feed auth is dropping.
- **`schwab_streamer_down`** on 07-27, 08-03, 08-10, 08-11 (recovered each time).
- **`schwab_standby` on many days** — a second data-service instance running. `memory/schwab-streamer-single-instance.md` states data-service MUST be 1 replica. Two long-lived pods are live.
- **Stuck orders**: the dashboard shows MNQU6 sell orders from **2026-08-13T21:30 still "Working"** a week later.
- **`multi-strategy:state` last written 2026-04-15** — four months stale.

## 6. Verdict

The book is **not yet fundable**, and the reason is plumbing, not alpha — exactly the conclusion of
the June 2026 audit repeating. A 37% fire rate on the highest-frequency strategy means the live book
cannot reproduce its backtest regardless of whether the backtest is right.

**Fix order:** (1) why PCC skipped 12 sessions — conditioner gate or fault; (2) data-service down to
one replica and fix Schwab auth refresh; (3) clear/---explain the stuck 08-13 working orders;
(4) confirm signal price is sampled at the stamp, not at the decision bar; (5) only then judge P&L,
and only after ~60+ signals.
